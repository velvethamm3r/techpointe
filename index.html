How I work

Four rules, written into every engagement letter

One client per category

On retainer, one company per category: one buyer, one budget, named in your letter. Sprint clients get confidentiality instead.

No lobbying

I don't contact Congress or senior officials for clients. I introduce you to program offices and buyers, and you make the case.

Conflicts checked

I check for conflicts before I take any engagement.

Client work stays separate

Each client's files are kept apart, and nothing I learn from one is used for another.
