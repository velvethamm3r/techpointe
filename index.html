01

Public trust

Monthly · quoted after a scoping call

A company in a scrutinized category that policymakers, journalists and enterprise buyers understand and trust.

You get: message architecture, two pieces drafted and pitched a quarter, briefing materials your team delivers, an incident-comms plan on the shelf.

Measured by: inbound from the right people; accuracy of coverage.

02

Regulatory readiness

Monthly, or a fixed fee per workstream

The rule or attestation that would have stalled a deal, handled before the deal.

You get: a quarterly risk register (AI Action Plan, EU AI Act, state laws, FedRAMP, export controls, FCC Covered List) and one readiness workstream, run with your counsel or assessor.

Measured by: questionnaire turnaround; findings closed before diligence.

03

Federal go-to-market

Monthly · quoted after a scoping call

Pilots and conversations with the Department of War, CISA, DARPA, the national labs and the primes, months sooner than you'd get them alone.

You get: target-account map, procurement-path memo, introductions where the map shows a fit, prep and debrief for every meeting.

Measured by: conversations started, time to pilot, pilots started.

Offers combine. Selling into government usually needs Federal go-to-market plus Public trust; raising a round usually needs Regulatory readiness.
